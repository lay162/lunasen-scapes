Why families and schools book us

Party hire that does not overwhelm

Standard hire assumes every child wants noise, crowds and bright flashing everything. Plenty of children do not, and that is who we set up for.

Quieter by design

Extended air hoses let us site the blower well away from the bouncing area, and we can run a lower-noise unit where the venue allows it.

Colour you choose

Pick a calm white or pastel layout, or a high-contrast primary set if strong visual cues help. Both are in stock, so the choice is yours rather than ours.

Access thought through

Wide step-up entries, floor-level soft play, room for a wheelchair beside the mats, and a quiet corner set apart from the main run.

Unhurried setup

We arrive early and build before guests are in the room, so nobody has to sit through a noisy install or a rushed changeover.

Properly covered

£5m public liability and PIPA-tested inflatables through King of the Castles. Certificates go to your venue or school office on request.

Told once, remembered

Tell us what sets your child off and what settles them. It goes on the job sheet and the crew reads it before they knock on the door.

How booking works

Four steps, no pressure

  1. Tell us the day

    Send the date, the postcode and roughly how many children. A rough idea is plenty to start with.

  2. We suggest a setup

    You get a written quote with the equipment, the space it needs and any sensory adjustments included.

  3. Confirm and relax

    We hold the slot, note the access details and agree an arrival window that suits the household.

  4. We build it quietly

    Early arrival, clean equipment, a safety brief for the adults, then collection at a time that is not mid-meltdown.
