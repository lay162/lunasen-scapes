Before delivery day

Choose the set by need, not by trend

Lowest visual load

The luxury white set removes patterns and competing colours. It suits calm corners, christenings and children who find busy layouts hard to process.

Clear visual boundaries

Single-colour and two-colour sets make the edge of the play zone easier to read. High-contrast primary shapes can support tracking and sorting.

Indoor measurements

Measure the usable floor after tables, fire exits and doorways are accounted for. We scale the mat layout to the room rather than blocking access.

Local hire questions

Before you book

Is soft play suitable for an indoor hall?

Yes. It needs usable floor space but no blower or ceiling clearance. We keep fire exits, doorways and accessible routes clear.

Is the equipment cleaned between hires?

Yes. The wipe-clean vinyl is cleaned and checked between bookings. Tell us about any specific hygiene requirement when you enquire.

Can I choose the colours?

Yes. White, pink and lilac, yellow and white, orange and green, and primary-colour sets are shown in the live catalogue. Availability is confirmed for your date.
