Areas we cover

Our regular delivery routes run across the Wirral peninsula, Merseyside and Cheshire. Outside those, we still deliver — we are just honest about travel time and any cost before you commit.

Where we go every week

CH — Wirral, Chester & Ellesmere Port

CH41 to CH49 and CH60 to CH66. Birkenhead, Wallasey, New Brighton, Prenton, Bebington, Heswall, West Kirby, Hoylake, Bromborough, Neston, Willaston, Little Sutton, Ellesmere Port and Chester.

L1 to L40. Liverpool city centre, Allerton, Woolton, Childwall, Aigburth, Speke, Halewood, Huyton, Kirkby, Bootle, Crosby, Maghull and Formby.

WA — Warrington, Widnes & St Helens

WA1 to WA16. Warrington, Widnes, Runcorn, St Helens, Newton-le-Willows, Lymm, Frodsham and Northwich fringe.

CW & SK — Cheshire

CW1 to CW12 and SK9 to SK11. Crewe, Nantwich, Northwich, Winsford, Middlewich, Sandbach, Wilmslow, Alderley Edge, Handforth, Poynton and Macclesfield.

Delivery and setup

Delivery, setup and collection are included in every quote. We give you an arrival window rather than a vague half-day, and we build before guests arrive.

What we need on site

A reasonably flat surface, access wide enough to get the equipment through, and a power socket within roughly 25 metres for anything inflatable. Tell us the access route and we will tell you straight away if it will work.

Outside the area?

Our sister company King of the Castles delivers nationwide from a Manchester base. Between us there is usually a way to make it work.

Covered? Let us hold your date

Weekend slots in school holidays go early. Send the date and postcode and we will confirm availability.
