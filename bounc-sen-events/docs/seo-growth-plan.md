# Local SEO growth plan — Bounc-SEN Events Ltd

## The honest target

The site is technically ready to compete for:

- SEN bouncy castle hire Wirral
- SEN soft play hire Wirral
- sensory soft play hire Wirral / Liverpool / Merseyside
- bouncy castle hire Wirral
- soft play hire Wirral
- bouncy castle and event hire Merseyside
- bouncy castle and soft play hire Cheshire
- inclusive event hire North West
- school and community fun-day hire with an SEN quiet zone

Nobody can guarantee position one. Local results depend on searcher proximity, Google Business
Profile strength, reviews, website relevance, links/citations, business history and the competitors
Google chooses for that query. The goal is measurable growth in qualified impressions, map views,
calls and quote requests — not a promised number that no supplier controls.

## What the competitor and sister-company audit showed

Search-visible King of the Castles pages target a **service + location** or **service + audience**
combination:

- `bouncy-castle-hire-north-west`
- `cheshire-bouncy-castle-hire`
- `school-and-event-fun-day-liverpool`
- corporate fun days
- 360 photo booths
- generators
- themed party packages

Its indexed copy also links the wider niches together: bouncy castles, assault courses, interactive
games, rodeo attractions, side stalls, slides, furniture, generators, photo booths, weddings,
schools and corporate events.

That is why this site now has five substantial local service guides. They answer different
questions instead of generating dozens of thin pages with only the town name swapped:

| bouncy castle hire Wirral | `/bouncy-castle-hire-wirral.html` |

The local competitor results show the same pattern. PG Bouncy Castle Hire and PK Bouncy Castle Hire

soft-play page. Bounc-SEN's defensible difference is not another list of town names: it is specific,
credible SEN practice — blower siting, colour choice, floor-level access, predictable setup and a
separate calm zone.

## Postcode strategy

The coverage page includes all 14 principal North West postcode areas:

| Zone | Areas |
| --- | --- |
| Core Wirral / Merseyside / Cheshire routes | CH, L, CW, WA |
| Regional partner routes | M, WN, SK, PR, BL, OL, BB, FY |
| Extended-distance routes | LA, CA |

The checker deliberately does **not** say every address is automatically covered. It explains which
route the postcode falls into and says that date, equipment, access and travel price need written
confirmation. That distinction protects the business from a broad SEO claim becoming a customer
promise.

Do not create one near-identical page for every postcode. Google's doorway-page guidance treats
large groups of pages made only to funnel similar local queries as spam. Add a new local page only
when it has genuinely local content: real jobs, venue/access advice, delivery detail, original
photos and customer questions from that area.

## Backlink audit — what can and cannot be known

A public web search found one clear external citation to King of the Castles:

- Build An Event directory — “King of the castles entertainment”

Search results are **not a complete backlink database**. A proper link inventory requires access to
Google Search Console for owned domains and, optionally, a paid index such as Ahrefs, Majestic or
Semrush. Do not describe a public search as a complete backlink audit.

### Highest-value first link

King of the Castles should link to the new site from a real sister-company paragraph on a relevant
page and, if appropriate, its footer. Suggested copy:

> Looking for a lower-stimulation setup? Our sister company
> [Bounc-SEN Events Ltd](https://bounc-sen-events.co.uk/) provides SEN-inclusive bouncy castle,
> soft play and sensory event hire across Wirral, Merseyside and Cheshire.

Use the brand name as the main anchor. Do not repeat exact-match anchors such as “bouncy castle hire
Wirral” sitewide; that looks manufactured. One relevant editorial link is more useful than a page of
keyword anchors.

Also add Bounc-SEN to the King of the Castles `sameAs` / organisation relationship if its platform
supports structured data, and link back to the appropriate King of the Castles service page from
the North West guide. The Bounc-SEN site already makes the relationship clear.

## Citation and link acquisition order

Keep the exact same business name, phone, service-area address format, domain and opening hours on
every listing.

1. **Google Business Profile** — highest local priority; guide in `google-business.md`.
2. **Bing Places** and **Apple Business Connect** — same NAP and canonical domain.
3. **Facebook page** — set the website field to the canonical domain and complete the About section.
4. **King of the Castles** — relevant sister-company editorial link as above.
5. **SEND/local-offer directories** — Wirral Local Offer, Liverpool Family Information and SEND
   directory, Cheshire West Live Well / Local Offer, and Cheshire East Live Well. Apply only to
   directories whose eligibility the business genuinely meets.
6. **Venue and school partners** — ask halls, SEN groups, nurseries and charities that have actually
   used the service to list Bounc-SEN on their suppliers/resources page.
7. **Industry and local business citations** — Yell, Thomson Local, FreeIndex and relevant event
   directories, with identical NAP. Quality and consistency matter more than quantity.
8. **Local press and community organisations** — a real launch, accessible-fun-day partnership or
   donated quiet zone is newsworthy; a generic “please link to us” email is not.

Avoid paid link packages, private blog networks, automated directory blasts, comment links and
guest posts on unrelated sites. Those links are easy to buy because they carry little value and can
create a manual-action risk.

## Google Business Profile alignment

Use one service-area profile for the real Wirral operation. Google allows up to 20 service areas and
recommends that the overall boundary usually remain within about two hours' driving time. The exact
20-place list is in `google-business.md`.

Do not put keywords into the business name unless they are part of the registered/real-world name.
Use the services, description, posts, photos and website pages for relevance.

Priority profile work:

1. Verify the profile.
2. Replace every website placeholder phone/email/domain before connecting it.
3. Add each real item and package as a service.
4. Upload the supplied logo and all product photos.
5. Add new photos from real setups, with permission, every month.
6. Ask every genuine customer for an honest review within 48 hours of collection.
7. Answer every review; do not copy-paste keyword-heavy replies.
8. Publish a useful post roughly every two weeks: availability, new equipment, weather guidance,
   hall measurements, setup examples or an SEN adjustment explained plainly.

## Search Console and measurement

After the final domain is live:

1. Verify the domain property in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect and request indexing for the home page, hire page and five local guides.
4. Check **Pages** for canonical, crawl or structured-data errors.
5. Check **Performance** monthly by page and query. Record impressions, clicks, average position and
   quote conversions — do not react to daily rank movement.
6. In **Links**, export the real external-link report. This becomes the baseline backlink audit.
7. Connect Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the same sitemap.

Use this tagged URL in Google Business Profile:

`https://bounc-sen-events.co.uk/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`

If analytics is added, implement consent correctly before loading non-essential tracking. Search
Console itself does not require a visitor cookie banner.

## Content that will move the site forward

Only publish material backed by real experience and original photos:

- a real Wirral setup, including venue type, access and why the layout was chosen
- “bouncy castle or soft play when blower noise is a trigger?”
- indoor hall measuring checklist
- weather and outdoor-inflatable safety
- quiet zones at school and community fun days
- choosing low-stimulation versus high-contrast colour schemes
- a real event where Bounc-SEN and King of the Castles equipment worked together

Each article should answer a customer question, link to the relevant equipment and area page, and
end with one clear quote action. Do not manufacture reviews, case studies, locations or clinical
claims.

## Monthly operating checklist

- Confirm NAP and hours are still identical everywhere.
- Add at least one real-job photo to Google Business and Facebook.
- Ask recent customers for honest reviews and respond to existing ones.
- Check Search Console indexing and top queries.
- Fix broken links and out-of-stock product copy.
- Record new venues/towns actually served; use that evidence to improve area pages.
- Pursue one genuine local partnership or citation rather than dozens of low-quality links.
