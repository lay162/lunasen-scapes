Local hire questions

Before you book

Do you cover every North West postcode?

All 14 principal North West postcode areas can be quoted, but that does not mean every date or item is automatically available. Cumbria and Lancashire routes may carry a longer-distance delivery charge. The written quote is the confirmation.

Who supplies the larger event equipment?

The quote states whether Bounc-SEN Events Ltd, King of the Castles or both are supplying the booking, so insurance, equipment and crew responsibility are clear.

Can an event have both high-energy and quiet attractions?

Yes. That is the point of the partnership: the main event can have large attractions while a properly separated SEN-aware zone remains available throughout.
