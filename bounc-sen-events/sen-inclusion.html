Home/SEN inclusion

The Bounc-SEN inclusion standard

Traditional party hire is loud, bright, fast and built for a crowd. For a lot of neurodivergent children and young people, that is the reason they end up watching from the kitchen door. This page sets out exactly what we do differently, so you can judge it for yourself rather than take our word for it.

Six commitments

What we change, on every booking

These are not paid extras and you do not have to ask for them. They are the default.

Noise kept down

Extended air hoses put the blower metres away from the bouncing bed instead of right beside it. Soft play, ball pits and rockers need no blower at all, so a fully quiet setup is genuinely possible.

Visual load you control

All-white, single-colour and high-contrast primary sets are all in stock. Low stimulation and strong visual cues are both valid needs, and we do not assume which one you want.

Physical access first

Floor-level soft play, wide step-up castle entries and clear transfer space beside the mats. We plan the layout around how your child moves, not around how the photo looks.

Predictable timings

An arrival window, not a vague morning. Built before guests arrive, collected when the room is calm. If the day slips, we wait rather than rush a pack-down in front of an upset child.

A calm corner by default

Every setup can include a quiet zone away from the main run — a rocker, a ball pit or just matting and a boundary — so there is somewhere to go that is not 'outside'.

Clean, tested, covered

Wipe-clean vinyl cleaned between every hire, PIPA-tested inflatables, and £5m public liability through King of the Castles. Certificates on request, no chasing.

What to mention when you enquire

None of it is compulsory and none of it is nosy. It just means the crew turns up already knowing.

  • Sounds, textures or colours that are a hard no
  • What helps your child settle when it gets too much
  • Whether a stranger in the room is a problem, and how you would like us to arrive
  • Mobility, transfers and anything that affects getting on and off equipment
  • Ceiling height and doorway width if we are coming indoors
  • Whether you want the setup finished before anyone else arrives

Straight answers

Questions we get asked a lot

What actually makes a setup 'SEN friendly'?

Three things, mostly: noise, visual load and pace. We site the blower as far from the bouncing area as the hose allows, we let you pick a calm or high-contrast colour scheme instead of defaulting to rainbow, and we build before guests arrive so nobody has to sit through the install. Everything else we adjust to the child.

Can you run a bouncy castle quietly?

Not silently — a castle needs a blower running the whole time, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something. What we can do is use an extended hose to put the unit several metres away, choose a siting that puts a wall between the blower and the child, and suggest soft play instead if noise is the main barrier. Soft play needs no blower at all.

My child uses a wheelchair or a walking frame. What can they use?

Soft play, ball pits and the sensory rocker all work at floor level, and we leave clear transfer space beside the mats so there is room to get in and out. Castles need a step-up entry, so tell us about mobility when you enquire and we will be honest about what will and will not work at your venue.

Do you set up indoors?

Yes. Soft play, ball pits and rockers go indoors anywhere with the floor space. Castles need ceiling height, so send us the room height along with the floor dimensions and we will confirm before you commit.

Are you insured, and can our school see the paperwork?

Yes. Public liability cover of £5m and PIPA-tested inflatables come through our sister company King of the Castles. Ask and we will send the certificates and a risk assessment straight to your school or venue office before the booking date.

What if my child cannot cope on the day?

It happens, and it is not a problem. Tell the crew — they will hold off, give it time, or pack down quietly without an audience. Let us know in advance what helps and what makes it worse, and it goes on the job sheet.

Do you charge more for SEN adjustments?

No. Quiet-blower siting, colour choice, layout changes and a slower build are how we work, not extras. What changes the price is the equipment, the hire length and how far we travel.
