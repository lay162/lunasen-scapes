Before delivery day

Planning a Cheshire booking

Hall height

For an indoor castle, send the clear ceiling height at the intended position, not the venue's headline room height. Lights and beams reduce usable clearance.

Rural and gated access

Mention long carries, gravel drives, coded gates and limited parking. Accurate access information keeps the arrival window realistic.

Mixed-age events

Pair a castle or larger attraction from the King of the Castles Cheshire range with a separate soft-play and sensory area so younger children and anyone needing a quieter space have a real option.

Before you book

Which Cheshire postcodes do you serve?

CH, CW and WA districts are regular routes; SK availability depends on the specific district and date. Use the checker and send the full postcode for confirmation.

Can you provide documents to a Cheshire venue or school?

Yes. Ask for public liability, PIPA certificates and the relevant risk assessment when you request the quote.

Do you provide event hire as well as children's parties?

Yes. Wider inflatables, interactive games, rodeo attractions, furniture and event equipment can be combined through King of the Castles.
