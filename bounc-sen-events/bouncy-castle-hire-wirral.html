Before delivery day

What we check before a Wirral garden setup

Ground and anchoring

Real grass is normally the simplest outdoor surface. Tell us beforehand about artificial grass, concrete, slopes, drains or recent landscaping so we can confirm whether safe anchoring is possible.

Access

Send the narrowest gate or passage width and mention steps, alleyways and parking restrictions. This avoids an inflatable arriving that cannot reach the garden.

Weather

Wind and rain decisions are safety decisions, not sales decisions. We check the forecast and will not operate an inflatable outside its tested limits.

Local hire questions

Before you book

Which Wirral postcodes do you cover?

Our core Wirral route covers CH41 to CH49 and CH60 to CH66. Enter the outward code on our areas page or send the full event postcode for a written delivery quote.

Can you put a bouncy castle on artificial grass or concrete?

Sometimes, but never by assumption. Safe anchoring depends on the inflatable, surface and site. Send a photo and dimensions and we will confirm what is possible.

Can the blower be made quieter?

A running inflatable cannot be silent. We can use the available hose length and the site layout to place the blower farther from the child; soft play is the no-blower option.
